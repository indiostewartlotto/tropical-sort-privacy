Account & Data Deletion — Tropical Sort

Developer: Mr. Pineapple Studios  ·  App: Tropical Sort  ·  Last updated: 2026-05-28

This page explains how to request that your Tropical Sort account, and the data associated with it, are deleted. We comply with deletion requests within 30 days.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — Inside the app (recommended)

  1. Open Tropical Sort.
  2. Tap the Settings icon on the home screen.
  3. Scroll to the Account section.
  4. Tap Sign out, then tap Delete account in the confirmation sheet.
  5. Confirm. Your account and all associated data will be queued for deletion within 30 days.

Option 2 — By email

If you've uninstalled the app or can't access the in-app option, email us from the address associated with your Google sign-in:

  • Email: indiostewartlotto@gmail.com
  • Subject: "Delete my Tropical Sort account"
  • Body: Please include the email address of the Google account you used to sign in, so we can find your data.

We'll confirm receipt within 3 business days and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What data is deleted

Everything tied to your Google account will be permanently removed from our backend (Supabase) within 30 days:

  • Your profile — coin balance, lives, streak counter, daily-reward chain.
  • Your level progress — which levels you've cleared, star ratings, best move counts.
  • Your speed records — best times per level.
  • Your unlocked achievements — badge history.
  • Your authentication identity — the Supabase auth record linking your Google account to a Tropical Sort player ID.

You'll be signed out of the app, and the next time you open Tropical Sort, you'll start fresh as a new anonymous player (or sign in again to create a new account).

What data we retain, and for how long

A small amount of data is retained for legal or operational reasons, but all of it is anonymized — it can no longer be linked back to you after your account is deleted:

  • Aggregated gameplay analytics (e.g., "X% of players cleared Level 47") — retained for up to 12 months in anonymized form for game-balance analysis. No personal identifier remains.
  • AdMob ad-serving data — managed entirely by Google AdMob under Google's own privacy policy. We don't store this on our backend.
  • Tax / payment records — if you've made an in-app purchase, the transaction record (no personal data beyond the purchase amount and product) is retained by Google Play under Google's policy and by us for tax compliance, typically 7 years per Caribbean / EU tax law. This record cannot identify you.
